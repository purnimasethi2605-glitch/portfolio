Policy Analyst, Northfield Regional Council (made up example posting)

The Policy Analyst conducts quantitative and qualitative research to support
policy development. You will collect and analyze economic data, prepare
briefing notes for senior leaders, and present findings to stakeholders.

Responsibilities
Conduct research and data analysis on regional economic trends.
Prepare briefing notes, reports and presentations for decision makers.
Build dashboards in Excel and Power BI to track program performance.
Engage stakeholders and community partners on policy options.
Support program evaluation using regression analysis in R or Stata.

Qualifications
Degree in economics, public policy or a related field.
Knowledge of regression analysis and statistical software such as R or Stata.
Clear written communication, including briefing notes for senior leaders.
